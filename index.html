Why v1 is exactly this size

Can't be smaller

No episode runs → breaks the promise for ~75% of viewing[6]

No personalization → just a runtime filter

No end time → the member still does the math

No no-match state → empty screens for those who need it most

Shouldn't be larger

Ads plan needs ad-load prediction first

Combos and co-viewing add logic before we validate the basics

Every extra platform delays the A/B test. Fixed team, one-quarter target: scope is the variable we control

Top risks, and how we de-risk them

01Low adoption, like Play SomethingUX/ProductEvening banner at the decision moment. Kill criterion: repeat usage below 10% → pause
02Wrong end timesTechEnd-credits markers, round up to 5 min, exclude titles without metadata
03Weak options in short windowsUXRelevance threshold + no-match state (FR-17)
04Members watch less overallBusinessGuardrails on evening hours and retention. "Keep watching" always available

Backup · Non-functional requirements

PerformanceOptions within 2 s at p95. Banner adds ≤ 100 ms to home load at p95
ReliabilityPersonalization timeout (3 s) → fallback list by runtime and local popularity; never blank. Feature failure never affects browsing or playback. End time accurate within 5 min for ≥ 95% of options
Usability≤ 3 remote actions to playback. Existing TV patterns, living-room readability, same screen-reader support
PrivacyOnly data already processed under the current policy. No new personal data. Last window stored per profile
Legal & localeOnly licensed, maturity-permitted titles. 12/24-hour end times by locale. Localized UI
CompatibilityUnsupported TV app versions hide the entry point. Reuse existing services, no new content pipeline

Constraints & assumptions

Plans. Ad-free only: ad breaks make end times unpredictable.

Platform & market. TV app, US & Canada, highest revenue per member [10].

Metadata. Reliable runtime and end-credits markers exist for almost every title.

Device. Most planned evening viewing happens on a TV.

Behavior. End times assume uninterrupted viewing; v1 does not re-plan after pauses.

Backup · Monetization

Questionv1 decision
PricingNo price change, no separate charge. Part of the existing subscription
PackagingIncluded in every ad-free plan in launch markets. Not on the ad-supported plan in v1
Paid add-onRejected. Netflix packages value through plans [2]; a paid add-on shrinks reach and adds friction to a feature built to remove it
Vs. the ads planNot marketed as a reason to leave it. The ads plan drove 60%+ of sign-ups in its markets in Q1 2026 [1]. The exclusion is technical and temporary
Business valueIndirect: faster starts and completed evenings support engagement quality and retention [1]
Buying processNo purchase flow. Appears automatically for eligible profiles via the evening banner. No locked teaser
